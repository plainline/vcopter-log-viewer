VCopter Log Viewer

Decode ZeroZero VCopter .logbin flight logs. Everything runs in your browser -- your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Drop your PTZ + RawData .logbin files here, or click to choose them.

Either file works alone too (with reduced info) — this tool figures out which is which by content, not by filename.
